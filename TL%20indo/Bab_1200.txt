**Chapter 1200: No One Can Save Heaven from Collapse**

Many years ago, when Yi Shu first learned of Duyu Sect's theory, he was like most Taiyan Sect cultivators—absolutely dismissive.

When calamity came, instead of actively seeking solutions, they chose to hide like turtles and place hope in some vague savior waiting for the calamity. It was completely baseless, unpredictable variables.

However, among the Ten Immortal Dao Sects, all tracing back to Taidu the Grand Master, Duyu Sect's legacy appeared since ancient times. To exist this long means there must be genuine reason behind it.

Only outsiders cannot see Duyu Sect's mysteries.

Today, when Yi Shu arrived at Great Qi Saint Dynasty—a dynasty seemingly born from a crack in stone—he witnessed technologies rivaling Wanxian Alliance's finest, along with new methods completely different from current cultivation paths. These new teachings could completely undermine the foundation of Chuanfa Venerable in Xuan Huang Realm.

Instantly, Yi Shu recalled what Duyu Sect had once said.

Back then, the Great Calamity had just begun. Core members of Ten Immortal Dao Sects gathered to discuss possible countermeasures.

Every sect expressed their views, yet Duyu Sect remained extremely passive.

"Distinguished ones, what they call 'escaping disaster is inevitable.' If struggle could avoid it, then what would you call calamity?" Lu Chengheng, Duke of Duyu Sect, kept his eyes closed and nose focused on heart, acting detached.

"This is your Duyu Sect's excuse for placing those so-called [Guanshi Divine Eyes] everywhere?"

Hou Xia Yan, Lord of Wuding Prison, whose temper was the most irritable, frowned and immediately countered.

"Hou, you don't understand. This is necessary to wait for [Xuan Huang Dukman] to appear." Lu Chengheng spoke calmly.

"Damn Xuan Huang Dukman! In face of calamity, all beings perish. Once everyone dies, where will this savior spring from?" Hou nearly cursed aloud.

"Do you question the Grand Master's legacy?" One sentence from Lu Chengheng silenced Hou Xia Yan completely.

"But you are still here, aren't you?" Lu Chengheng smiled, then added meaningfully, "Xuan Huang Realm won't be destroyed so easily. If any cultivation realm can survive till the end within this star sea, surely it is our Xuan Huang."

When the ten sect leaders heard this, none replied.

After brief silence, perhaps to soothe everyone, Lu Chengheng briefly explained: "Duyu concept isn't baseless. Quantity calamity and birth-destruction are part of heaven-earth cycle. Even Immortal World calamity may well be predicted by True Immortals."

"You're all anxious day and night. What about the Grand Master? Does He show even one flicker of disturbance?"

Xuán Ji, head of Taishang Sect who maintained silence throughout, finally spoke: "Grand Master's merit participates creation itself. Even in Immortal Ruins He walks like flat ground. The calamity destroying Xuan Huang Realm, He doesn't care. We can't. Lu Brother, if you choose reclusion, I won't stop you. But regarding confronting calamity, you can't contribute zero effort."

Seeing everyone's gaze turn toward him, Lu Chengheng finally nodded and made compromise.

But Lu Chengheng's final words left everyone silent again.

"One day, you'll know we Duyu Sect were right."

Now, crossing thousands of years, Taiyan Sect's collective consciousness and Yi Shu's body suddenly remembered Lu Chengheng's words once more.

After rudely understanding principles of [Build Foundation Using Myself], Huada Stone Mother incorporated this variable into future deduction of Xuan Huang Realm.

Originally clear future, as though hit by chaotic storm, became obscure and unclear.

Even with Stone Mother's currently god-like computational power, she momentarily felt somewhat lost.

Taiyan Sect actually once tried deducing new cultivation methods capable of overthrowing transmission systems.

But...

Creating something from nothing differs fundamentally from forecasting future based on existing data.

Huada Stone Mother only judged such possibility does exist. But probability is too small.

Even smaller than survival probability of Chuanfa under Lifelong Realm siege back then.

Beyond wanting to create new methods themselves, more importantly is Chuanfa's pervasive influence over Xuan Huang Realm.

Just as cultivators gradually forget Xuan Huang Realm disasters while infinitely ignoring geological changes occurring across thousands of years in Xuan Huang Realm...

Deducing new methods isn't merely extreme expression of creativity.

It's also contending against Chuanfa's influence.

Thus, in Huada Stone Mother's deduction clearly aware of Chuanfa Venerable's current strength, probability of new methods emerging naturally approached infinite zero.

Yet, events far exceeding deductive possibilities exactly happened.

In remote corner of Xuan Huang Realm drifting void, still unknown to anyone, within a recently established faction.

New methods not only quietly emerged but already developed to Golden Core stage!

Truly earth-shattering, causing even Huada Stone Mother's deductions to stutter slightly.

Because according to all currently known factors, none can identify why this point in time allowed new methods to emerge.

Only explaining through Xuan Huang Dukman outside normal reasoning, outside variables, works.

Within secret discussions among Ten Sects, they also once discussed the Xuan Huang Dukman.

Resulting in near-unified conclusion: If such Dukman truly exists, either comes from outside Xuan Huang Realm, or is reincarnation of some powerful figure.

Native cultivators growing solely by oneself power, attempting to hold back heaven collapsing...

Totally impossible.

No matter how gifted, still won't work.

Xuan Huang Realm environment no longer creates fundamental soil capable of resisting Chuanfa Venerable.

"What exactly is background of this Great Qi Faceless Holy Emperor?"

Now, Yi Shu, or rather Taiyan Sect, has completely lost contempt.

Instead, they treat Great Qi Saint Dynasty as equal, even higher position than themselves.

And they're genuinely deducing possibilities of forming alliance with Saint Dynasty.

As former ruler of Xuan Huang Realm, now compressed by Wanxian Alliance under Chuanfa Venerable to only Taiyuan Small World, impossible that Taiyan Sect above and below feels no resentment.

Even no longer needing territory, resources, such grievance still remains like nightmare in Taiyan Sect consciousness, never dispersing.

"Former great dao sects, Taishang Sect and others, though appearing to resist Wanxian Alliance outwardly, have secretly already merged with them, equivalent to Chuanfa's dogs."

"But certainly like us, they haven't fully submitted either. Missing is only opportunity."

Appearance of Great Qi Saint Dynasty, especially emergence of new methods, let Taiyan Sect glimpse this sliver of hope.

However, these being sects surviving from Ancient Calamities.

Making any choice concerning fate requires utmost caution.

(This chapter ends)
